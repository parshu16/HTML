the day we met

Aarav took the same bus to work every morning, and every day he saw Meera sitting quietly by the window with a blue notebook in her hands. For months, they never spoke, but their eyes often met, 

followed by shy smiles
. One rainy morning, Meera slipped while getting onto the crowded bus, and Aarav caught her hand. That simple moment became the beginning of something beautiful. They started talking every day, sharing their dreams, fears, favorite songs, and little secrets. Aarav eventually gave Meera a poem he had written for her, ending with the words, “You make mornings beautiful.” Meera smiled and confessed that she had been taking the 8:10 bus even though another bus was faster. When Aarav asked why, she held his hand and said, “Because you were on it. ” Years later, they still remembered that rainy morning as the day their lives changed forever. Their love had begun quietly, with a simple smile and a helping hand, but it grew into a love that neither of them could imagine living without.
